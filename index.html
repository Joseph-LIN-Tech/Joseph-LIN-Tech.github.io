I am an AI Researcher at Alaya Lab, with current research interests in model acceleration for world models and video generation for AI-native game scenarios.

I received my Ph.D. degree in March 2026 from the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, The University of Tokyo (UTokyo), under the supervision of Prof. Yinqiang Zheng. I obtained my M.S. degree from UTokyo in March 2023. Prior to that, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), from 2019 to 2021. I received my B.Eng. degree from the South China University of Technology (SCUT).

Hiring! We are looking for self-motivated interns to join us in Tokyo. Students whose interests align with efficient modeling and acceleration for world models and video models in gaming scenarios are welcome to send their CVs to my email.
